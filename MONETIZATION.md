# Monetization status

No ads, payments, tracking, or earnings have been activated. The owner has no AdSense account yet. Public guide and privacy-overview pages provide useful context, but do not guarantee eligibility or approval.

## Before activation

1. Resolve hosting: the current Vercel Hobby plan is non-commercial only. Choose commercial-permitted hosting or explicitly approve a paid Vercel plan before earning from the deployment. No upgrade has been requested or performed.
2. The owner must create an AdSense account at https://adsense.google.com/start/ using their own identity and payment information. Do not share passwords or payment details in chat.
3. Choose the final public domain and submit the site through AdSense. Ownership verification and Google review are required; account creation alone does not approve the site.
4. Supply the real publisher ID, approved ad-unit configuration, and an owner-approved public contact address. Add an accurate contact/privacy policy and consent configuration before activating ads. The current privacy overview describes the app but is not a complete advertising privacy notice.
5. Use only substantive public editorial pages for candidate ad placements. Google forbids ads where private communication is the page's primary focus. Do not load AdSense on create, unlock, reveal, sender management, or success screens. Do not put a global Auto Ads script in the app shell. Keep public pages as separate full-document navigations so an ad script cannot persist into the secret UI.
6. Add the publisher's actual verification code and ads.txt entry after the hosting decision; never publish fabricated publisher IDs. Configure Google's certified consent flow for applicable regions before any ad requests. Do not treat a generic cookie banner as an approved CMP.
7. Check placement, crawler accessibility, consent, and ad isolation before publishing. Earnings depend on approval, audience and eligible ad demand; there is no guaranteed amount.
